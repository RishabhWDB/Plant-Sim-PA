Season Report:

The monsoon season was a challenging and unpredictable experience. The farm's growth score averaged 0.56, with 37 days of score below 0.5. The season ended on day 120 with a final progress percentage of 48.77.

* I initially focused on maintaining soil moisture levels, but failed to account for the monsoon's impact on humidity, leading to frequent score drops.
* I intervened 55 times, mostly to adjust irrigation and humidity levels, but these interventions were often too late to prevent score drops.
* I noticed that the farm's growth score improved when soil moisture levels were between 30-50% and humidity was around 60%.

What I Would Do Differently Next Season:
