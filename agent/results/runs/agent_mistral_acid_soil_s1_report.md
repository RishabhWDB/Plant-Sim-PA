The season started with a soil pH of 4.9 and 50% soil moisture. I focused on gradually increasing pH and maintaining optimal soil moisture. The crop grew steadily, reaching a growth score of 0.94 by day 15. However, on day 33, the score dropped to 0.03 due to a sudden pH decrease to 4.17 and high humidity. I intervened by adjusting pH and humidity, but the crop took time to recover. Key decisions included frequent pH adjustments and monitoring soil moisture. Next season, I would implement a more robust monitoring system to detect and respond to sudden changes in pH and humidity more quickly. Additionally, I would consider using a buffer to stabilize pH levels and prevent drastic fluctuations.
